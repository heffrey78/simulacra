## Evidence pulled from the world file afterwards

From `saves/world.db`, the world this session was played in:

- **All five floors got a mirror motif** from the director, and floor names
  repeated — "Echoes of the Forgotten" on floors 1–2, "Echoes of the Shattered
  Veil" on 3–4, and "Echoes of the Shattered Veil II" on 5 — despite the
  director being told the previous names.
- **Every one of the ten `search` discoveries was keyed on the room's own
  name** (`{'lair', 'forgotten'}`, `{'narrowing'}`, `{'portal', 'lost'}` ...):
  the parser fallback filled in the room name as the target, and M10 then
  generated a second description of the room and filed it as a find.
