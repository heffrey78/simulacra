## Bugs

**Taking what a search just described.**

```
> search
The brass scales hang suspended like cold birdcage traps, ...
> take scales
There is no scales here.
```

The earlier run did the same with a boot scraper. M12's "part of the room" rule
reads the room's prose, not its discoveries.

**Narration spoken as dialogue.** `the Widow: The Widow takes it.`

**Room names in braces.** `{The Start}`, `{The Hallway}`, `{Nothingness}` — the
2b's formatting, which the director passes through. "Nothingness" also escapes
the stock-word list.

**"is here." on every greeting.**

```
the Assayer is here.
> greet Assayer
the Assayer is here.
the Assayer: I am valued at four dollars per ton, ...
```

**An unreadable `drink`.** `Use what? You have a tin of peaches, a canteen, still
heavy, a tin of peaches, a canteen, still heavy.`

**Floor 1 has no name in the transcript.** Floors 2–5 open with *"You descend to
floor 2. The Weight of Stone"* and a goal; floor 1 has neither, because the REPL
ignores `FloorNamed`.

## The model

- **Longer prose.** Median 64 words a room against 50 on the 1.7b's runs, max
  116, against a two-sentence brief. Inventions: *"Room 301"*, *"your family
  vanished years ago"*, *"a Tuesday afternoon"*, sky and sun in a mine. `glass`
  seven times.
- **NPCs speak in the first person (M13 held) and garble their canon.** The
  Assayer: *"I am valued at four dollars per ton"* — a number it invented. The
  Widow: *"You do keep a plate warm because I am a Widow…"* Hardpan's canon is
  written without a subject — *"Keeps a plate warm for a shift that ended…"* —
  under "What is true of you:", so the model supplies "You".
- **The 1.7b echoed its instructions** in the earlier run, twice: *"You are a
  rogue assayer, unbound by conventional rules…"* — the NPC system prompt's "You
  are the Assayer, who…", read back. The guard looks for prompt labels, and this
  has none.
